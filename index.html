Image → Roblox RGB converter

Recommended: choose the image file saved on your iPad. This avoids image-host restrictions. Maximum output is 255 × 255 pixels (65,025 blocks); tall or wide images are resized to fit.

If you cannot select a saved image, you can try the direct URL below, but some image hosts block conversion.
